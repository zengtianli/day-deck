REVIEW ACCESS
Use the dedicated demo account and password in the App Review Information fields. Open the fourth tab “连接” (Connection), find “审核演示 · 独立合成数据”, enter those two fields, and tap “连接审核服务”. This connects to our live, independently authenticated review service containing synthetic examples only. No personal gate password or DEBUG launch argument is needed. The app does not offer public account registration.

CORE FLOW
“今天” shows overdue/today/later tasks; open a task to inspect its details and change its status. “通知” opens available dates, a summary and expandable timeline; search filters that day's records. “随手记” saves a note to the review service and confirms success only after a valid server receipt; failure preserves the draft. The review account permits these real read/write operations. “连接” → “退出审核并返回个人云端” revokes the review session, removes its saved login and returns to the separately authenticated personal service. Review data/drafts use separate storage and are never sent to a paired Watch.

“设置与更新” on Connection offers a persistent startup-page preference, configuration import/export/backup, optional iCloud configuration sync (off by default; startup page only), and Apple version lookup. Update installation is handled by App Store/TestFlight. Credentials, diary drafts, notification data and reminder permissions are excluded from configuration sync. Adding a system reminder is an explicit user action requesting EventKit access; reminders are not uploaded to our service. The app sends no notifications or badges.

INDEPENDENT PURPOSE AND SHARED CODE
Notihub is the mobile client of our notification review workspace: existing authorized service users can review tasks, dated summaries and timelines away from their Mac, and write brief notes. It belongs to the same product family as Notihub Mac, sharing our own brand/icon and cloud data rather than collecting another copy of notifications on the phone. One mobile project covers iPhone/iPad/Vision and a companion Watch summary. The Mac collector has different permissions/lifecycle and cannot serve as this touch/watch client.

Mobile business models, agenda grouping, server receipts, recovery, reminder mapping and views are maintained in this product. Developer-shared configuration/lifecycle/update/diagnostic modules provide infrastructure only; they do not bundle another app's business data. The sanitized source is MIT-licensed at https://github.com/zengtianli/notihub/tree/main/ios. The client uses Apple frameworks without advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Examples are synthetic; personal content is fetched at runtime and never included in the bundle. This is the developer's own product, not a client template submission.
